Pharmacy Module — Documentation

A single guide for everyone: pharmacists, store managers, cashiers, and developers. This is a UX prototype with dummy data — all actions are demo-only.

What is this app?

This is the pharmacy management module for a retail medical store (built for a Telangana, India pharmacy). It handles the full cycle of running a pharmacy: buying stock from suppliers, receiving and tracking it by batch & expiry, selling to customers (walk-in and prescription), and staying compliant with drug-license and GST rules.

New here? Read Who uses it and A typical day first, then jump to the guide for whichever tab you're on.

Who uses it (roles)

💊
Pharmacist
Sells medicines, dispenses prescriptions, verifies Schedule H drugs, handles returns. Lives mostly in Sales and Rx Dispense.
💼
Store Manager
Orders stock, receives deliveries, monitors reorder & expiry alerts, reconciles cash, runs reports. Uses Purchase Orders, GRN, Overview, Reports.
🤖
Developer
Implements this UX. See Handoff notes for data flow, role-gating, and what each demo action should really do.
Role access: Settings and Audit Trail are Org Admin only. Cashiers/pharmacists should not see them. The developer will gate these by role.

A typical day (end-to-end)

1
Open the store — check Overview: reorder alerts, expiring batches, pending prescriptions.
2
Reorder low stock — raise a Purchase Order (medicine + qty) and send to the supplier.
3
Receive a delivery — record it in GRN with batch, expiry, cost & MRP. This creates stock.
4
Sell all day — walk-in sales in Sales; doctor prescriptions in Rx Dispense.
5
Handle exceptions — Returns (customer/supplier), Damages (breakage/expiry), Stock Adj. (count mismatch).
6
Close the day — cash reconciliation on Overview, and generate Reports (GST, Schedule H, stock register).

Core concept: stock, batches & FEFO

This is the single most important idea in the app:

Stock is tracked at the batch level, and the source of truth is GRN. A product's on-hand quantity is derived, not typed.

Products.stock = SUM(GRN batches) − Sales − Damages ± Adjustments

  • Products is the catalog — what medicines exist (name, MRP, GST, schedule). It does not hold real stock.
  • GRN (Goods Receipt) is where stock enters — each received line creates a batch with its own expiry and cost.
  • FEFO (First Expiry First Out): at sale time the system auto-picks the batch nearest to expiry, so old stock sells first.
  • Expired batches are blocked from sale and should be moved to Damages or returned to the supplier.

Telangana / India compliance

RequirementWhat it means in the app
Schedule H / H1 drugs HPatient name & age are required and the sale should link a doctor's prescription. Logged in the Schedule H register (Reports).
Drug licenseStore DL, pharmacist registration & validity stored in Settings; shown on invoices & the Overview bar.
GSTCGST + SGST split for intra-state (Telangana). GST report is filing-ready.
Stock registerOpening/closing quantities are auditable (Reports → Stock Register).
Selling priceCannot exceed printed MRP.
Audit trailEvery add/edit/delete/void is logged with who & when.
OTC vs Schedule H: ordinary over-the-counter sales can be anonymous. The name/age requirement kicks in automatically only when a Schedule H item is in the cart.

Overview (Dashboard)

Who: everyone. Purpose: the store at a glance.

  • KPIs (products, suppliers, customers, invoices), today's revenue.
  • Reorder alerts — items below their reorder level, with a one-click Auto-Order.
  • Expiring soon — value exposure from batches nearing expiry.
  • Schedule H audit log & pending prescription queue.
  • Day-end cash reconciliation — enter physical cash vs expected.

Products (Catalog)

Who: store manager / admin. Purpose: the master list of medicines you sell.

1
Click + Add, fill name, form, schedule, HSN, MRP, GST, reorder level.
2
Save — the product joins the catalog. A row also shows its batches & a stock audit.
Import Catalog bulk-loads the product master from Excel — it does not add stock. Stock always comes from GRN.

Suppliers

Who: store manager. Your distributors, wholesalers & manufacturers. Add them here so they're selectable when raising a Purchase Order or recording a GRN. Keep GSTIN accurate for purchase tax records.

Customers

Who: pharmacist / manager. Registered/repeat customers for credit, loyalty and prescription-linked sales. Walk-in buyers don't need to be added — their name is entered directly at the Sale counter.

Purchase Orders

Who: store manager. Purpose: tell a supplier what you want to buy.

1
Pick the supplier, add lines (medicine name + quantity only — you don't know batch/expiry/cost yet).
2
Save & Send (or export Excel/PDF) to the supplier. A new PO row appears in the list.
3
When goods arrive, use Receive against this PO to pre-fill a GRN.

GRN — Goods Receipt

Who: store manager. Purpose: record what physically arrived. This is where stock is created.

1
Select supplier, enter their invoice number & date.
2
For each line enter batch, expiry, quantity, cost & MRP straight from the supplier invoice (prior values auto-fill as hints).
3
Save GRN & Update Stock — creates dated batches and increases on-hand stock. Use Save as Partial if only some items arrived.
Import from Supplier Invoice bulk-loads a large delivery from Excel — this import adds stock (unlike the Products catalog import).

Sales

Who: pharmacist / cashier. Purpose: bill a walk-in sale in four steps.

1
Customer — type a walk-in name, or switch to Registered Customer and pick one.
2
Add products — search shows live stock & the FEFO batch; expired/out-of-stock items are blocked. Set qty, click + Add to Line Items.
3
Line items — review; batch is auto-picked (editable). Remove any line.
4
Payment — choose method; for cash, enter amount tendered to see change. Finalize & Pay, or Hold to park the bill.
Schedule H: if an H drug is in the cart, name & age become mandatory and you should link the prescription.

Rx Dispense

Who: pharmacist. Purpose: fill prescriptions sent by doctors.

1
Open a pending prescription from the queue.
2
Verify each item, tick the Schedule H verification, pick batches (FEFO).
3
Dispense & Generate Bill — creates an invoice stamped with the Rx for the audit trail.

You can also start from Sales and use Link Prescription to pull a prescription's patient & medicines into the sale.

Returns

Who: pharmacist / manager. Two opposite flows:

Sale ReturnPurchase Return
DirectionCustomer → youYou → supplier
ReferenceOriginal invoiceOriginal GRN
StockBack in (or write-off to Damages)Removed
MoneyRefund customerDebit note from supplier

Damages

Who: pharmacist / manager. Record stock that can no longer be sold — broken ampoules, spillage, expired batches. Pick the specific batch; on-hand stock is reduced and it feeds the expiry-loss figure on the dashboard.

Stock Adjustments

Who: manager. Use only when the physical count differs from the system (miscount, theft, found stock). Enter a + or − with a reason. For breakage/expiry use Damages; for deliveries use GRN.

Reports

Who: manager / admin. Pick a report, set a date range, then Generate or export CSV:

  • Sales & Purchases (GRN) summaries
  • GST — CGST/SGST for filing
  • Schedule H Log — mandatory restricted-drug register
  • Expiry — batches nearing expiry with value
  • Stock Register — license-mandated opening/closing record

Audit Trail Admin

Who: admin only. A read-only, tamper-evident log of every create/update/delete/void/login — who, what, when, and before→after values. Filter by user, module, action, date, or record. Used for accountability, disputes and inspections.

Settings Admin

Who: admin only. Store-wide configuration: pharmacy & license details, tax/GST, invoice & printing, inventory rules (reorder level, expiry window, FEFO/FIFO), compliance toggles, notifications.

For developers — handoff notes

Data flow (must-honor)

Products.stock = SUM(GRN batches) − Sales − Damages ± Adjustments

  • Products import = catalog master only, never stock.
  • GRN import/entry = the only thing that creates stock (as batches).
  • Sale deducts from a specific batch (FEFO default, override allowed). Block expired batches.
  • PO carries name + qty only. GRN adds batch/expiry/cost/MRP.

Role gating

  • Settings & Audit Trail: Org Admin only.
  • Cashier/pharmacist: Sales, Rx Dispense, Returns, Overview (read).
  • Store manager: adds Products, Suppliers, PO, GRN, Damages, Stock Adj., Reports.

Compliance rules to enforce server-side

  • Require patient name + age (and Rx link) when a Schedule H/H1 item is billed; write to Schedule H register.
  • Block selling above MRP and selling expired batches.
  • Write an immutable audit-trail entry on every mutating action.

🔄 Two kinds of returns. Sale Return: a customer brings medicine back — reference the original invoice; stock goes back in (unless expired/damaged). Purchase Return: send goods back to the supplier against a GRN (short-expiry, wrong item) — creates a debit note and reduces stock.
